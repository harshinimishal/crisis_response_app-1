# 🚨 Crisis Response App

A smart mobile application designed to assist users during emergency and disaster situations.  
The Crisis Response App provides tools for **quick emergency communication, safety awareness, and disaster preparedness**, helping users get assistance even in difficult situations.

The app integrates **Bluetooth connectivity, sensor-based safety features, emergency alerts, and disaster information** to improve response time and increase personal safety.

---

# 📱 Features

## 🚨 Emergency SOS System
- One-tap **SOS emergency trigger**
- **SOS Countdown Timer** before sending the alert to prevent accidental activation
- Automatically sends **emergency messages to registered emergency contacts**
- **Live location sharing** with emergency contacts

---

## 📍 Live Location Sharing
- Share real-time location with trusted contacts during emergencies
- Helps responders track and reach the user quickly

---

## 📡 Bluetooth Connectivity
- Detect nearby devices using **Bluetooth**
- Enables communication in **low or limited network environments**
- Helps identify nearby users who may assist during emergencies

---

## 🛟 First Aid & CPR Guide
- Built-in **first aid instructions**
- **CPR guide and emergency response steps**
- Provides immediate guidance until professional help arrives

---

## 🗺️ Safety Map
- Displays nearby **safe zones and important locations**
- Helps users navigate toward safer areas during emergencies

---

## 🚑 Responders List
Quick access to important responders such as:

- Hospitals
- Ambulance services
- Fire departments
- Police stations
- NGOs and emergency organizations

---

## 📢 Disaster Alerts & Notifications
- Receive notifications about **natural disasters or emergency situations**
- Helps users stay informed and take preventive action

---

## 🛡️ Safe Mode
Safe Mode provides additional safety monitoring features and can assist users in risky situations.

---

## 📊 Sensor-Based Safety Features
The application uses device sensors to enhance safety monitoring, such as:

- Motion detection
- Environmental awareness
- Activity monitoring for potential emergency conditions

*(Exact implementation may vary depending on device capabilities.)*

---

## 👤 User Profile & Settings
Users can manage their personal information through full **CRUD operations**.

Features include:

- Create and update profile details
- Add, edit, and remove **emergency contacts**
- Manage safety preferences
- Customize notification settings

---

## ✉️ Emergency Contact Messaging
When an SOS alert is triggered, the system:

1. Starts the **SOS countdown**
2. Sends an **emergency message to all registered emergency contacts**
3. Shares the user's **live location**

---

## 📶 Connectivity Support
The application is designed to work across multiple connectivity conditions:

- Internet connectivity
- Bluetooth-based device detection
- Limited network environments

---

## 📖 App Tutorial
The app includes a built-in **tutorial/guide** to help users understand:

- How to trigger SOS alerts
- How to add emergency contacts
- How to access safety features
- How to use the safety map and responder tools
